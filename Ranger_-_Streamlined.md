* **Hunt & Track (WİS):** When you **follow a trail of clues left behind by passing creatures**, roll+WİS. On a 7+, you follow the creature’s trail until there’s a significant change in its direction or mode of travel. On a 10+, you also choose 1:

  * Gain a useful bit of information about your quarry, the GM will tell you what.

  * Determine what caused the trail to end.

* **Called Shot:** When you **attack a defenseless or surprised enemy at range**, you can choose to deal your damage or name your target and roll+DEX.

  * **Head** – 10+: As 7–9, plus your damage. 7-9: They do nothing but stand and drool for a few moments.

  * **Arms** – 10+: As 7-9, plus your damage. 7-9: They drop anything they’re holding.

  * **Legs** – 10+: As 7-9, plus your damage. 7-9: They’re hobbled and slow moving.

* **Command:** When you **work with your animal companion** on something it’s trained in...

  * and you attack the same target, add its **ferocity** to your **damage**.

  * and you track, add its **cunning** to your **roll**.

  * and you take damage, add its **armor** to your **armor**.

  * and you **Discern Realities**, add its **cunning** to your **roll**.

  * and you **Parley**, add its **cunning** to your **roll**.

  * and someone **İnterferes** with you, add its **instinct** to their **roll**.

##Advanced Moves (Levels 02 To 10)

_When you gain a level from 02-10, choose from these moves._

* ☐ **Friends Of The Wilderness:** You had contact with individuals from several races in the wilderness. You gain the elf starting move if you took the human one at character creation or vice-versa.

* ☐ **Wild Speech:** You can speak with and understand animals. At the level 6 or higher, you can speak with and understand any non-magical, non-planar creature.

* ☐ **Hunter’s Prey:** When you **Spout Lore about a monster** you use WİS instead of İNT. At the level 6 or higher, on a 12+, in addition to the normal effects, you get to ask the GM any one question about the subject.

* ☐ **Viper’s Strike:** When you **strike an enemy with two weapons at once**, add an extra damage for your off-hand strike based on your level: +1d4 for levels lower than 6, +1d8 for the level 6 onwards.

* ☐ **Camouflage:** When you **keep still in natural surroundings**, enemies never spot you until you make a movement.

* ☐ **Man’s Best Friend:** When you **allow your animal companion to take a blow that was meant for you**, the damage is negated and your animal companion’s ferocity becomes 0. İf its ferocity is already 0 you can’t use this ability. When you have a few hours of rest with your animal companion its ferocity returns to normal.

* ☐ **Blot Out The Sun:** When you **Volley** you may spend extra ammo before rolling. For each point of ammo spent you may choose an extra target. Roll once and apply damage to all targets.

* ☐ **Well-trained:** Choose another training for your animal companion.

* ☐ **God Amidst The Wastes:** Dedicate yourself to a deity (name a new one or choose one that’s already been established). You gain the commune and cast a spell cleric moves. Your effective cleric level is one level lower than your own level.

* ☐ **Follow Me:** When you **Undertake A Perilous Journey** you can take two roles. You make a separate roll for each. At the level 6 or higher, roll twice and use the better result for both roles.

* ☐ **A Safer Place:** When you set the watch for the night, everyone takes +1 to 
**Take Watch**. At the level 6 or higher, in addition, after a night in camp when you set the watch everyone takes +1 forward.

##Advanced Moves (Levels 06 To 10)

_When you gain a level from 06-10, choose from these moves._

* ☐ **Beast’s Belly:** When you **know your target’s weakest point** your arrows have 2 piercing.

* ☐ **Observant:** When you **Hunt & Track**, on a hit you may also ask one question about the creature you are tracking from the Discern Realities list for free.

* ☐ **Special Trick:** Choose a move from another class. So long as you are working with your animal companion you have access to that move.

* ☐ **Unnatural Ally:** Your animal companion is a monster, not an animal. Describe it. Give it +2 ferocity and +1 instinct, plus a new training.
