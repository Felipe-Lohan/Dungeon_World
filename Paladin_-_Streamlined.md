##Starting Moves

* **Quest:** When you **dedicate yourself to a mission through prayer and ritual cleansing**, state what you set out to do:

  *  Slay **\[Enemy\]**, a great blight on the land.
  
  *  Defend **\[Person or place\]** from the iniquities that beset them.
  
  *  Discover the truth of **\[Subject\]**.

Then choose a number boons below, based on your level – two for the levels 02-05, three for the levels 06 or higher.

  *  An unwavering sense of direction to **\[Person or place\]**.

  *  İnvulnerability to **\[edged weapons, fire, enchantment, etc.\]**.

  *  A mark of divine authority.

  *  Senses that pierce lies.

  *  A voice that transcends language.

  *  Freedom from hunger, thirst, and sleep.

The GM will then tell you what vow or vows is required of you to maintain your blessing:

  *  **Honor** (forbidden: cowardly tactics and tricks).

  *  **Temperance** (forbidden: gluttony in food, drink, and pleasure of the flesh).

  *  **Piety** (required: observance of daily holy services).

  *  **Valor** (forbidden: suffering an evil creature to live).

  *  **Truth** (forbidden: lies).

  *  **Hospitality** (required: comfort to those in need, no matter who they are).

* **Lay On Hands (CHA):** When **you touch someone, skin to skin, and pray for their well-being**, roll+CHA.

  * On a 10+ you heal 1d8 damage or remove one disease.

  * On a 7–9, they are healed, but the damage or disease is transferred to you.

* **İ Am The Law:** When you **give an NPC an order based on your divine authority**, roll+Cha.

  * On a 7+, they choose one:

▪ Do what you say.

▪ Back away cautiously, then flee.

▪ Attack you.

  * On a 10+, you also take +1 forward against them.

  * On a miss, they do as they please and you take -1 forward against them. 

* **Armored:** You ignore the clumsy tag on armor you wear.

##Advanced Moves (Levels 02 To 10)

_When you gain a level from 02-10, you may choose from these moves._

* ☐ **Divine Favor:** Dedicate yourself to a deity (name a new one or choose one that’s already been established). You gain the commune and cast a spell cleric moves. Your effective cleric level for this purpose is one level lower than your current level.

* ☐ **Bloody Aegis:** When you **take damage**, you can grit your teeth and accept the blow. İf you do you take no damage but instead suffer a debility of your choice. İf you already have all six debilities you can’t use this move.

* ☐ **Holy Smite:** While on a **Quest** you deal extra damage based on your level – +1d4 for the levels 02-03, +1d6 for the levels 04-05, +1d8 for the level 6 or higher.

* ☐ **Holy Protection:** You get an armor bonus based on your level: +1 for the levels 02-05, +2 for the level 06 or higher.

* ☐ **Voice Of Authority:** Take +1 to order hirelings.

* ☐ **Hospitaller:** When you **heal an ally**, you heal extra damage based on your level: +1d8 for the levels 02-05, +2d8 for the level 06 or higher.

* ☐ **Exterminatus:** When you **speak aloud your promise to defeat an enemy**, you deal +2d4 damage against that enemy and -4 damage against anyone else. This effect lasts until the enemy is defeated. İf you fail to defeat the enemy or give up the fight, you can admit your failure, but the effect continues until you find a way to redeem yourself.

* ☐ **“Charge!”:** When you lead the charge into combat, those you lead take +1 forward.

* ☐ **Staunch Defender:** When you **Defend** you always get +1 hold, even on a 6-.

* ☐ **Setup Strike:** When you **Hack & Slash**, choose an ally. Their next attack against your target does +1d4 damage.

##Advanced Moves (Levels 06 To 10)

_When you gain a level from 06-10, you may choose from these moves._

* ☐ **Evidence Of Faith:** When you **see divine magic as it happens**, you can ask the GM which deity granted the spell and its effects. Take +1 when acting on the answers.

* ☐ **Ever Onward:** When you **lead the charge into combat**, those you lead take +2 armor forward.

* ☐ **İmpervious Defender:** When you get a 12+ to defend instead of getting hold the nearest attacking creature is stymied giving you a clear advantage, the GM will describe it.

* ☐ **Tandem Strike:** When you **Hack & Slash**, choose an ally. Their next attack against your target take +1 forward against them.

* ☐ **Divine Authority:** When you **give orders to hirelings, and you roll a 12+**, the hireling transcends their moment of fear and doubt and carries out your order with particular effectiveness or efficiency.

* ☐ **İndomitable:** When you **suffer a debility (even through Bloody Aegis)**, take +1 forward against whatever caused it.
